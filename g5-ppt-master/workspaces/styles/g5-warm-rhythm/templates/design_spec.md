# G5 Warm Rhythm — Style Specification

> Method and design defaults only. No project communication contract, brand identity, page structure, or SVG prototypes.

## I. Style Overview

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Style Name | G5 Warm Rhythm |
| Best Fit | Nonprofit fundraising decks, board and general-assembly reports, conference talks on education and family policy, course briefings for parents and young learners, partnership proposals |
| Reusable Intent | Let the audience feel why the work matters on dark emotional beats, then see the evidence and the next step on light content pages, so the deck both comforts and directs |
| Sources | User-authored house presentation specification (`pptx-house-style`), sections on brand rhythm, cards, and content ethics; transcribed 2026-11-01. Field-tested on a board-meeting deck and fundraising decks in 2026 |

## II. Communication Method

- **Preferred Mode**: custom
- **Mode References**: narrative, pyramid
- **Mode Behavior**: Use the narrative arc for the deck's emotional spine — a concrete child, family, or community moment sets the stake, the tension names what the current system fails to provide, and the resolution is what the organization does about it. Inside each evidence chapter switch to conclusion-first: the page title states the finding, the body supports it. Fundraising and proposals lean narrative; board reports lean pyramid with only a light emotional frame at opening and close.
- **Argument Flow**: Open on a dark emotional beat that names the human stake; move into light content pages that carry the evidence, mechanism, and plan; return to a dark beat at each chapter turn and at the close. Every critique of a policy or institution is paired with a concrete alternative on the same or the next page — criticism never ends a chapter.
- **Page Message Discipline**: One idea per slide. Content-page titles are full-sentence claims, left-aligned and large (30pt or more). If a page needs two takeaways, it becomes two pages. Write for a high-school reader: a technical term gets a plain-language gloss at first use.
- **Claim Discipline**: Keep data, field observation, theory, and inference visibly distinct. Theory (for example Bourdieu, Vygotsky, Ostrom) is cited by name only where it changes how the audience reads the evidence, never as ornament. An inference that is not yet backed by data is labeled as inference in the notes. Speaker notes carry spoken cues and pacing marks — where to slow down, where to leave silence — and are never duplicated onto the slide.

## III. Page Role Vocabulary

| Role | Communication Job | Evidence Obligation | Composition Tendency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emotional opening | Make the audience feel the human stake before any data | A de-identified, rewritten scene or voice; never a real name, face, or traceable detail without consent | Full dark field, one short line set large, generous silence, a thin warm-gold rule |
| Chapter turn | Mark a shift in the arc and reset attention | State the question the next chapter answers | Dark field, chapter number or short phrase, almost nothing else |
| Evidence page | Show one finding with its support | Source and period stated on the page; unverified figures stay as visible placeholders | Light field, claim title, one chart, table, or card group carrying the proof |
| Mechanism page | Explain how the program or model works | Steps or parts drawn from actual practice, not idealized | Light field, a short sequence or relationship diagram on paper-tone cards |
| Voice page | Let a parent, child, or partner be heard | Quote is de-identified and rewritten for privacy; attribute only by role and age band | Paper-tone quote block, ample margin, no portrait unless consent is documented |
| Ask page | State exactly what is requested of this audience | Amounts, accounts, and contacts only when confirmed; otherwise placeholders | Light field, the ask as the dominant element, one clear next action |
| Closing | Return to the human stake and sign off | Echo the opening scene; no new claims | Dark field, literary display title, warm-gold short rule, signature line |

## IV. Evidence & Data Expression

- **Argument Trace**: Each evidence page traces back to the stake named on the opening beat and forward to the ask or plan. A page that serves neither is cut.
- **Charts**: Native, editable charts only. Label directly on the marks, state units and period, and annotate the one reading the audience should take away. Primary series in the brand's main color; comparison series in its secondary color; the single highlighted value in its accent color.
- **Tables**: Use for budgets, schedules, and side-by-side comparisons. Keep one row shape; right-align numbers; mark which figures are audited and which are estimates.
- **Sources**: Cite the source and date beside the figure it supports. Laws and policies are cited in their currently effective version. Taiwanese-context years use the Republic-of-China calendar on the slide when the audience is domestic (for example `一一五年`).
- **No Fabricated Figures (binding)**: Financial and statistical figures are never invented, rounded into plausibility, or carried over from an example. Until the real value is in hand, the slide shows a visible placeholder such as `【待填入】` or `【待確認】`, and that page's speaker notes state that the placeholder must be replaced by the accountant-verified or source-verified figure.
- **Privacy (binding)**: Parent and child voices are always de-identified and rewritten; aggregate statistics never expose individual records. Donation accounts, QR codes, and contact details are placeholders until confirmed, and a deck with any such placeholder is not for external distribution.
- **Native Editability**: Keep every text, chart, and table as native editable objects so staff can correct figures after audit without redesigning the page.

## V. Visual System Defaults

- **Preferred Visual Style**: custom
- **Visual Style References**: soft-rounded
- **Visual Style Behavior**: Soft-rounded cards and gentle elevation on light content pages, alternated with full-bleed deep-green emotional pages. The alternation is the deck's signature rhythm; it follows the arc rather than a strict every-other-page pattern.
- **Composition**: Light content pages place a large left-aligned claim title at the top and one carrier below it. Dark emotional pages are sparse and centered on a single line or quote. Covers and closings share one composition so the deck visibly returns to where it began.
- **Density**: Low on emotional pages, moderate on content pages. Never compress two ideas onto one page to save a slide.
- **Decoration**: Rounded rectangles on a paper-tone surface with a soft shadow are the default container. Do not use colored edge strips or side bands to separate cards; separate by spacing and surface tone. A short warm-gold rule is the one recurring ornament — under cover and closing titles and at chapter turns.
- **Color Behavior**: Deep green carries emotional pages; white carries content pages; warm gold is rare and precise, reserved for the one thing to notice; the secondary blue supports comparisons. Any confirmed Brand identity supplies the exact values.
- **Typography Character**: A clear, humane sans for titles and body; a calligraphic literary face only for cover, closing, and occasional display titles on emotional pages. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from boxes or underlines.

## VI. Image & Icon Direction

- **Preferred Image Rendering**: warm-scene
- **Image Usage**: Images carry atmosphere on emotional pages (light through a window, hands at work, an empty classroom at dusk) rather than identifiable people. Content pages use images only when seeing the real thing prevents misunderstanding.
- **Image Treatment**: Keep faces turned away, blurred, or out of frame unless consent is documented. Allow a dark green scrim over photographs on emotional pages so text remains legible. Never place synthetic text inside generated images.
- **Icon Treatment**: One line-icon family at a consistent stroke, used to mark recurring page roles and list items. No decorative icon grids.

## VII. Review Focus

> Apply this section only after the user explicitly activates visual review. It never triggers that stage.

- Each page carries exactly one idea, and the content-page title states it as a claim.
- Dark emotional pages and light content pages alternate along the arc; no long run of light pages without a turn.
- No card uses a colored edge strip; containers are separated by spacing and surface tone.
- Every financial or statistical figure is either source-verified or a visible `【待填入】` / `【待確認】` placeholder with a matching speaker-note instruction.
- No real name, face, or traceable detail of a child or parent appears without documented consent.
- All on-slide text is Traditional Chinese with full-width punctuation; no Simplified characters.
- Cover and closing carry the literary display title, the warm-gold short rule, and the signature line.
- Speaker notes exist on every page and contain pacing cues, not a copy of the slide text.
